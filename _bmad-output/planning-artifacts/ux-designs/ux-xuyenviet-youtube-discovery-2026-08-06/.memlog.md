- (decision) Control Tower opens on an action-first queue, ordered by actionable candidate priority, stalled high-priority query needs, and persistent Discovery failures. Knowledge Mission and Automation Health are drill-in surfaces, not KPI-first default dashboard tabs.
- (decision) Discovery operations are desktop-first. Mobile supports health overview and basic candidate detail only; bulk review, query configuration, policy changes, and complex management remain desktop/tablet workflows.
- (decision) Desktop candidate review uses a ranked queue with a persistent selected-candidate inspector. This keeps priority scanning and safe decision detail in one workspace.
- (decision) Accept is immediate, without confirmation dialog. The UI enters pending state, then confirms that the URL was added to Knowledge intake and explicitly states capture is still a separate manual action; failure preserves the candidate and provides safe retry.
- (decision) Candidate review shows a plain-language recommendation plus 3-5 concise factors and explicit penalties. Full numeric model scores remain progressive disclosure for authorized operators and never appear as fact or credibility proof.
- (decision) Candidate decisions are one-at-a-time through the selected inspector. Bulk candidate actions are out of scope for the initial slice because Accept invokes Knowledge intake per URL.
- (decision) Channel/query blocking is out of scope for the initial Discovery UX. Operators can defer or skip a candidate; broader exclusion policy is deferred.
- (decision) Mission query management is one list of system-generated and operator-managed proposals. It shows origin, reason, priority, state, and next run; operators can create a simple query, edit text, reprioritize, and pause/resume. Advanced query-builder rules are deferred.
- (decision) Global Discovery toggle changes immediately without confirmation. The control and toast state clearly explain that disabling fences Discovery work only and does not change queued Knowledge sources or manual youtube:capture.
- (decision) Action queue contains only action-required groups: candidates needing review, stalled high-priority query/gap needs, and persistent Discovery failures. Normal deferrals and successful routine runs stay in Mission or Health history.
- (decision) After successful Accept, the inspector confirms the URL entered Knowledge intake and capture remains manual, the queue removes the candidate, and selection advances to the next ranked candidate. A toast provides a concise non-blocking confirmation.
- (change) Validation refinement: submitted and duplicate Knowledge intake outcomes have separate completed-handoff feedback; unknown Accept outcomes reconcile server state before retry. Queue advancement refetches ranked eligible candidates and falls back to completion state.
- (change) Validation refinement: desktop-first means optimized density, not missing functionality. At narrow/zoomed widths, Discovery reflows to sequential queue/detail views and all authorized functions remain available.
- (change) Validation refinement: Mission explicitly includes Coverage needs, Queries, Candidates, and Discovery funnel; Health explicitly includes schedule/state, throughput/backlog, incidents, usage telemetry, and safe affected-record drill-down. High-impact Knowledge recommendations route to their existing Knowledge review surface rather than resolving claims in Discovery.
- (change) Validation refinement: global switch displays fencing/cancelled/completed-before-cancellation run outcomes, disabled next-run semantics, and fresh re-enable semantics. Query/global states and Health projection freshness/unavailability are distinct.
- (override) User decision: channel/query/candidate blocking is deferred from the initial Discovery UX despite earlier proposal vocabulary. Initial operator actions are Accept, Defer, and Skip only; future exclusion policy requires a separate UX/architecture update.
